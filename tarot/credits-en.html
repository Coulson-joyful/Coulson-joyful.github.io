1909 Waite card art

The classic deck uses public-domain scans of the 1909 Rider–Waite Roses & Lilies edition illustrated by Pamela Colman Smith, from Wikimedia Commons; scan contributor: Saskia Jansen. It does not use modern recolorings, commercial packaging or modern card backs. Public-domain status remains subject to the laws of your region.

Meanings and correspondences

Imagery and meanings refer to A. E. Waite’s The Pictorial Key to the Tarot. Elemental and astrological correspondences separately refer to Golden Dawn’s Book T. Plain-language interpretations and question-matching rules are the project’s adaptation, not verbatim passages from those works.

Project-created content

Pink Dream, Night Vespers and Dream art were created for this project with AI assistance; icons were made for the project. The bilingual One Page Response is original project content written with AI assistance, not a copy or translation of a commercial Book of Answers. We claim no exclusive rights over traditional tarot symbols, public-domain works or third-party source material.

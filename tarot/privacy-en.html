Privacy policy

2026-10-26

Local operation

Card draws, spreads, astrology dice, PDF responses and history run on this device. We have no app accounts, advertising, analytics, external AI API or server receiving this content. Skin purchases and restoration use Apple store services and require connectivity. Cached valid purchase records can be verified offline. Source links and support email open in your system browser or mail app.

Information kept on your device

Your optional name and birth date, questions, card and dice results, readings, personal reflections, response-page snapshots, language and appearance preferences are stored locally by the app. Imported PDFs are copied into the app’s own file directory, with a 100 MB limit per file. We do not upload this content. Without a profile, the daily card uses a random seed saved locally. This seed is not uploaded or used for cross-app tracking.

Motion and on-device models

The daily draw may use motion sensors on request. Raw motion data is not sent to us. Supported devices can use Apple’s on-device language model to compose a reading; when unavailable, the app uses built-in interpretation rules. Questions and readings are not sent to a third-party AI service.

Purchases and Apple

Apple handles payment; we do not obtain your card number. Each paid deck is a non-consumable purchase: one payment, no auto-renewal. The app checks verified Apple transactions to determine purchased decks. Purchases do not transfer between different Apple accounts; a refund removes access to that deck. Apple’s own privacy policy governs its store, payment and system services. Optional tips are consumable purchases: each is a separate payment, with no auto-renewal or deck unlock, and cannot be restored.

Deletion and backups

Delete individual entries or clear history on the History page. “Erase history and recovery copies” in Library → Privacy policy also removes unreadable-history recovery copies. Deleting data does not revoke skin purchases; you can restore them after reinstalling. Delete your name and birthday in the local profile editor; delete the app’s imported PDF copy on the One Page Response page without changing the original. Response-page snapshots and text already saved in history remain until you delete those history entries. System backups, including iCloud device backup if enabled, may include app data and are controlled by system settings. Removing the Mac app itself may leave its data directory.

Contact and updates

For privacy or deletion questions, contact liuyf21@tsinghua.org.cn. When you email us, your address and message are sent via your chosen email service to that support mailbox; avoid unnecessary personal information. We will update this policy when the app’s data practices change.

Visiting this website

These public pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visitors’ IP addresses for security. This is separate from the app’s local storage; visiting these pages does not upload your app history, questions or PDFs. See GitHub’s privacy statement. These pages do not add analytics, advertising or forms.

Help and support

2026-10-26

Getting started

Start on Home with a daily card, astrology dice or a response page. Use Spreads for a complete reading. Interpretations offer a way to reflect on a question; they are not factual predictions or a substitute for medical, legal or financial advice.

Daily card and history

The saved daily card stays the same for that day. History has collapsible groups for daily cards, spreads, astrology dice and response pages. Changing language or card art does not draw new cards; saved original answer snapshots may retain their original language. A profile is not required for a daily card; name and birthday are optional. All four history categories let you record the situation, your next step and what happened later without changing the original reading. Clear all reflection fields and save to remove the reflection, or delete its history entry.

Seasonal spread

The seasonal spread appears three days before and after each equinox or solstice and can be drawn only on the event day, using Beijing time. It contains one major arcana card and one card from each suit. The current calendar covers 2026–2028; later years require an app update.

Skins and restoring purchases

Classic Waite art and core features are free. Pink Dream, Night Vespers and Dream are separate one-time purchases with no auto-renewal. Preview decks, open purchases or restore them in Appearance. Apple shows the actual price before purchase. Restore purchased decks with the same Apple account on supported devices. History stays on each device and does not automatically sync. Purchases have no usage time limit. A refund locks only that deck; history remains. “Support the developer” on the purchase page offers optional tips. Tips do not count as purchased decks or affect core features; each payment requires a separate confirmation.

Offline use and PDFs

Core features work offline; buying and restoring skins require connectivity. One Page Response includes 400 original responses in each language and can import a PDF you have permission to use. Scanned pages may have no extractable text; history still keeps a page snapshot. Deleting an imported copy does not delete your original PDF.

Backup and manual transfer

Export a file from History → History backup, then choose it from the same screen on another device and confirm the merge. All four history categories, reflections and response-page snapshots are included. Importing again does not add duplicates. Existing records and daily results for the same day take precedence. Backups do not sync automatically, restore purchases or include the full original PDFs you imported. Saved response pages remain available in history; import your original PDF separately to keep drawing from it. Each backup must not exceed 100 MB. Files are not encrypted, so keep them safe.

Reporting a problem

Email liuyf21@tsinghua.org.cn with your device, OS and app versions, the steps you took and what happened. Hide names, birthdays and private questions in screenshots. Never send your Apple account password or payment information.
